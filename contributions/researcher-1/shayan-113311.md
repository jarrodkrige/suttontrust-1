First‑generation students are less likely to finish university.
National longitudinal data show degree completion rates are around 4 percentage points lower for students whose parents did not attend university, even after taking prior attainment into account.
Source: UCL Centre for Longitudinal Studies / Nuffield Foundation (2021).
