First‑generation students are far less likely to attend Russell Group universities.
Longitudinal research tracking young people born in 1989–90 shows that students whose parents did not attend university were significantly less likely to enrol at a Russell Group institution than peers with graduate parents, even after accounting for prior attainment and background. This highlights how family educational background continues to shape access to the UK’s most selective universities. (UCL Centre, 2021)
