> Team 1 · Role accessibility-champion-1 · 2026-05-08 11:14

Use simple English everywhere
Short sentences, common words, one idea at a time. Avoid jargon and explain any unfamiliar terms.


State the purpose at the top of the page
Clearly say who the charity is, who it helps, and what users can do — in one heading and one sentence.


Pair icons with text labels
Icons can help, but always include text (e.g. ☎️ Call us, 📍 Find our centre), never icons alone.


Write buttons with clear actions
Buttons should say exactly what will happen, using verbs like Get help, Find support, or Donate food.


Design so translation tools work well
Avoid text inside images, leave space for longer translated words, and keep sentences short and clear.


Make help easy and welcoming to find
Use friendly language and obvious contact options, reassuring users that it’s okay to ask for help.
